# Materials and licenses

## New work in this update

- Orbital probe, station geometry, receiver, tether, stars and foreground fragments: original Canvas 2D code in `src/voyage/draw.ts`. No external model, texture, template or generated asset. Zero asset/API cost.
- Wind ambience: original deterministic procedural noise and filtering in `src/audio/atmosphere.ts`.
- Arrival, collection and transmission effects: original Web Audio synthesis in the same module. No samples or third-party sound recordings.
- New graphic and audio material is offered under CC0-1.0, following the existing portfolio audio license in `public/licenses/Portfolio-Audio-CC0.txt`.

## Retained portfolio material

- Existing NASA/SDO/AIA Sun image, courtesy of NASA/SDO and the AIA, EVE, and HMI science teams. Existing source and permission notes remain in localized `credits.html` and `docs/`.
- Existing procedural sky and constellation illustrations, original portfolio material.
- Project screenshots and demonstrations are the real existing application evidence. Existing captions, provenance and limitations remain intact.
- Onest and Manrope, locally hosted under SIL Open Font License 1.1, with license texts in `public/fonts/`.
- Atlas / quiet orbit original score, CC0-1.0. Existing score, provenance and license are retained in `public/audio/` and `public/licenses/`.
