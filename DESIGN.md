## State and sound

WorldState supplies active section, route progress, direction, smoothed velocity, pointer, proximity, receiver opening, quest, activation and independent sound/motion preferences to graphics and audio. Quest persistence is separate from reversible scroll state.

One opt-in AudioContext: music, ambience and SFX gain buses into master. Existing original stereo score is retained. Original mono noise is filtered into a quiet solar wind, controlled by measured velocity, with moderate stereo position. No context or score request occurs before consent. Scene pause does not mute audio; mute does not change motion. Volume controls all buses.

Arrival is a descending triangle pulse; collection is a two-tone optical chime; activation is a lower harmonic accent. Per-key cooldown prevents threshold chatter; SFX polyphony is capped at four. Final activation: 700ms preparation, music duck to 22%, accent, release into a settled state at 1.5s, then a 600ms music recovery. Hidden tabs suspend context and simulation. Unsupported audio leaves useful silent navigation.
