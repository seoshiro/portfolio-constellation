07 / PRODUCT DESIGN TOOLS

RESON

An object.
Your expression.

An original loudspeaker design studio. Configure materials and proportions, explore the construction in 3D, and compare named alternatives saved in your browser.

Open application ↗Source code ↗
  • TypeScript
  • Three.js
  • WebGL
  • LocalStorage

Independent project. Design, implementation and verification are documented in the source.

01

The starting point

Choose a cabinet shape, finish, driver trim, grille, base and concept dimensions. Save alternatives with unique names or import a validated JSON collection.

02

The result

A live original 3D concept, a reversible construction sequence, saved design thumbnails, side-by-side comparisons and a portable JSON collection.

03 / Case study

Under the surface

  1. 01

    Procedural Three.js geometry models a hollow enclosure, baffle, cone, surround, basket, magnet, rear terminals and optional wooden grille. The model remains a visual concept.

  2. 02

    Rendering runs when a view changes and pauses outside the viewport. Separate canvas areas keep explanatory text clear throughout forward and reverse scrolling, resize and orientation changes.

  3. 03

    Collections use bounded, validated data with reviewed replacement imports. Undo restores configuration and collection actions; storage failures keep export available.

04 / Case study

Practical boundaries

Illustrative appearance and construction only. No acoustic simulation, measured frequency response, electronic component selection or fabrication blueprint. Browser-local storage, six saved alternatives and JSON backups; no cloud sync.
