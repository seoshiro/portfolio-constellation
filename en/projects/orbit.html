02 / Educational tools

ORBIT

One spacecraft.
A different perspective.

A satellite mission studio: configure an original spacecraft, explore a circular orbit, reveal its construction, and compare locally saved studies.

Open application ↗Source code ↗
  • TypeScript
  • Three.js
  • Orbital mechanics
  • LocalStorage

Independent project. Design, implementation and verification are documented in the source.

01

The starting point

Choose a study preset, altitude and inclination. Switch the conceptual payload and blanket, fold the wings, control model time, and save named missions. Review JSON imports before merging or replacing local data.

02

The result

An interactive spacecraft and orbital plane, calculated circular speed and period, reversible construction chapters, and a portable local library with two-mission comparison.

03 / Case study

Under the surface

  1. 01

    The spacecraft, schematic Earth and orbit are original parametric geometry. Earth and the path share one scale; the moving spacecraft marker is enlarged and labelled for visibility.

  2. 02

    A circular two-body model uses NASA/JPL constants and independently checked equations. Inclination rotates the plane, including polar and retrograde orbits. The interface states its assumptions and distinguishes elapsed model time from real tracking.

  3. 03

    Saved studies have strict validation, atomic import, explicit replacement and undo. Blocked storage, corrupt data and changes from another tab are handled visibly. Keyboard controls, reduced motion, SVG fallback and context recovery preserve the learning workflow.

04 / Case study

Practical boundaries

An idealized educational model, with no drag, Earth rotation, telemetry, flight-readiness claim or power simulation. Desktop Chrome and emulated mobile contexts were tested; physical phones and Safari were not tested.
