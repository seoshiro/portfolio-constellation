08 / INTERACTIVE PRODUCT STUDIES

LUMEN

A lens.
Nine layers of motion.

An original optical lens study with a moving iris, an opening case, nine-group scroll-driven separation and a complete return to the assembled form.

Open application ↗Source code ↗
  • TypeScript
  • Three.js
  • WebGL
  • Scroll choreography

Independent project. Design, implementation and verification are documented in the source.

01

The starting point

Scroll naturally through the lens, use chapter controls or the keyboard, and explore the gallery in English, Russian or Kazakh.

02

The result

An original procedural lens and case, reversible assembly choreography, a moving iris and a responsive editorial study with a static fallback.

03 / Case study

Under the surface

  1. 01

    The lens, optical groups, moving iris and hinged case are original procedural geometry. No commercial model or manufacturer imagery is embedded.

  2. 02

    A shared scroll timeline separates nine modeled groups and reassembles them. Motion was reviewed continuously in both directions, including interrupted scrolling and viewport changes.

  3. 03

    Reduced motion, keyboard chapter controls, responsive captions and a no-WebGL fallback preserve access to the study. Local fonts and assets keep the published site self-contained.

04 / Case study

Practical boundaries

An illustrative fictional lens, not an optical simulation, manufacturing drawing or validated performance claim. Desktop Chrome and emulated mobile contexts were tested; physical-device Safari was not tested.
